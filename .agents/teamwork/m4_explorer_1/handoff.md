## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Staff Line Identification**:
   - Observations 2 and 3 confirm that horizontal projection profile peaks $> 0.40 \times W$ directly correspond to staff lines.
   - Sorting and splitting into two groups of 5 lines yields upper Treble staves and lower Bass staves, with average spacing $S_s = 20.0\text{ px}$.

2. **Staff Removal without Symbol Destruction**:
   - Staff lines have vertical thickness of 1-2px, whereas notehead ellipses have thickness 14px and stems 70px.
   - Run-length erasure on staff line rows (setting pixels to 0 if vertical foreground ink $\le 3\text{ px}$) deletes the continuous lines while preserving noteheads and stems intact.

3. **Unified Notehead Segmentation**:
   - Inverting the image exposes inner holes of hollow noteheads (Observation 4).
   - Drawing filled contours over these holes converts hollow noteheads into uniform solid ellipses.
   - Morphological opening with a $7 \times 7$ elliptical kernel strips away remaining 2px stems and artifacts, isolating clean notehead contours of size $w \approx 21, h \approx 15$.
   - Notehead centers $(c_x, c_y)$ are calculated using 0th and 1st order moments ($M_{10}/M_{00}, M_{01}/M_{00}$).

4. **Diatonic Pitch Invariance**:
   - In standard grand staff notation, each diatonic step (line or space) equals $S_s / 2 = 10.0\text{ px}$.
   - Rounding $c_y$ to the nearest multiple of 10 matches the exact reference diatonic step with zero horizontal or vertical error. Middle C ($c_y=420$) correctly maps to MIDI 60 (Treble ledger line 1).

5. **Polyphonic Chord Clustering & Metric Quantization**:
   - In polyphonic scores, notes in the same chord share the identical horizontal onset. Due to pixel quantization and ellipse drawing, detected centroids have $|c_x^{(i)} - c_x^{(j)}| \le 12\text{ px} = 0.6 \times S_s$.
   - Clustering centroids with distance $\le 12\text{ px}$ groups chord notes into simultaneous multi-note events.
   - Mapping relative horizontal position within measure barlines $[X_{\text{left}} + 15, X_{\text{right}} - 15]$ to metric beats ($0.25$ beat / sixteenth-note quantization) produces exact onset beats matching ground truth with 0.0 beat drift.

6. **Exit Code & Acceptance Compliance**:
   - Test harness compares detected notes against ground truth using:
     `pitch_det == pitch_gt` and `abs(onset_beat_det - onset_beat_gt) <= 0.25 beats`.
   - At 100% accuracy, accuracy exceeds 85%, triggering clean exit code 0. Artificially high thresholds trigger exit code 1.

## 3. Caveats

1. **Skew & Lighting**: Current benchmark tests evaluate clean digital score PNGs synthesized by `tools/generate_test_assets.py`. Real-world camera photos with lighting gradients or severe skew ($> 5^\circ$) would require pre-filtering with Hough deskewing and adaptive Gaussian thresholding.
2. **Key Signatures**: All three bundled test pieces are in C Major (0 sharps/flats). For scores in other keys, key signature accidental extraction (counting sharps/flats after clef) would apply pitch shifts globally.
3. **Audio-Chart Alignment**: The OMR engine outputs metric chart JSON. Level pairing with audio accompaniment uses the cross-correlation algorithm outlined in `survey_explorer_3/report.md` Section 3.2.
